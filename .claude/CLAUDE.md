# Blog writing rules

Posts live in `_posts/` and are published at https://signalandsawdust.com.

## Voice

- A sharp, friendly engineer talking to a teammate. Direct, a little
  irreverent, opinionated. Blog post, not whitepaper.
- First person, talk to the reader. Lead with the point, then explain.
- Mix short punchy sentences with longer ones.
- Bold the one phrase per section worth remembering. Sparingly.
- Be honest about tradeoffs and caveats. Dry humor is fine, forced jokes aren't.

## Don't

- No contrast-reveal framing: "It wasn't X. It was Y.", "Not because A, but
  because B", "The interesting part isn't X, it's Y", "That's their job. Yours
  is...", setup-then-twist closers or pull quotes. State the point plainly
  ("The rules caused the sprawl.").
- No corporate filler, AI hype, adjective piles, or exclamation points.
- No sycophancy or hedging everything into mush.
- Don't invent facts I didn't give you (timings, numbers, quotes). Leave a gap
  and ask.

## Formatting

- Front matter: `tags`, `title`, `layout: post`, `excerpt`. Recent posts open
  with a header image from `assets/`.
- Tables, blockquote pull quotes, and fenced code (including `diff`) all render
  well. Use them to break up long stretches of prose.
- Keep conclusions short.

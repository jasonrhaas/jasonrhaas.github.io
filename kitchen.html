Lipping and handles first, then the doors six at a time First cut

CUT -> LIP -> TRIM -> CHAMFER -> SAND -> BORE -> HANDLES -> OIL -> HANG. Sides keep the 1/8" reveal; top and bottom cover the full rail.

Handles 1 - table saw: rip the blanks

  1. Crosscut the 4-1/2" x 8 ft board into 2 pieces, 48" each.
  2. Straight-line rip one clean edge on each 48" piece and each 24-3/4" offcut and each 25" board to buy.
  3. 48" pieces: rip 1 blank, 2-1/4" wide, off each. Keep the strip left over on one.
  4. 24-3/4" offcut: rip 2 blanks, 2-1/4" wide, off each.
  5. 25" board to buy: rip 1 blank, 2-1/4" wide, off each.
  6. Stack them flat and let them move overnight.

Handles 2 - planer and router

  1. Plane the 5 new blanks and 1 strip to 7/8" thick. Feed them end to end, no gaps, to keep snipe off the ends.
  2. Route the profile on both edges of each blank while it is still wide.
  3. Route the profile on one edge of each strip.

Handles 3 - label the blanks

  1. 48" blank: C3 (door pair, right-most upper — cooking bowls)
  2. 48" blank: D7 (middle drawer, right of mini fridge) + D8 (bottom drawer, right of mini fridge) + C13 (door pair, under sink)
  3. 48" blank: C4 (door pair, left of microwave)
  4. 48" blank: C6 (door pair, right of microwave)
  5. 48" blank: D9 (middle drawer, right-most from mini fridge) + D16 (top panel, Zephyr fridge) + D6 (trash pullout, left of sink)
  6. 48" blank: C1 (door pair, above coffee station)
  7. 48" blank: C2 (door pair, middle upper — tupperware)
  8. 36" blank: D13 (middle drawer, right of stove) + D14 (bottom drawer, right of stove)
  9. 36" blank: D11 (middle drawer, left of stove) + D12 (bottom drawer, left of stove)
  10. 36" blank: C0 (door pair, above wine rack)
  11. 36" blank: D10 (bottom drawer, right-most from mini fridge) + D2 (top drawer, right-most from mini fridge)
  12. 25" blank: Bay0 (door pair, coffee station)
  13. 24-3/4" blank: C5 (door pair, above microwave)
  14. 24-3/4" blank: C7 (door pair, above Bosch refrigerator)
  15. 20" blank: spare
  16. 20" blank: spare
  17. Strip: D17 (bottom panel, Zephyr fridge).

Handles 4 - table saw: split the blanks

  1. Split all 14 blanks down the middle: two 1" strips each.
  2. Rip the strip to 1", profile edge kept.
  3. Tape each pair of strips back together - a door pair is cut from one blank.

Handles 5 - miter saw: cut every pull to length

  1. Cut both strips of a pair together, square to each other. This includes the 4 pulls already milled - they are not at length yet. Longest first.
  2. 42" × 8 - C2-L, C2-R, C3-L, C3-R, C4-L, C4-R, C6-L, C6-R
  3. 41-7/8" × 2 - C1-L, C1-R
  4. 32-3/4" × 3 - D4, D13, D14 (D4 is C2-R - already milled)
  5. 32-1/4" × 3 - D3, D11, D12 (D3 is C3-R - already milled)
  6. 29-3/4" × 2 - C0-L, C0-R
  7. 26-3/4" × 3 - D1, D7, D8 (D1 is C1-R - already milled)
  8. 26-1/4" × 2 - D16, D17
  9. 26-1/8" × 3 - D2, D9, D10
  10. 23-5/8" × 2 - C5-L, C5-R
  11. 23-1/2" × 2 - C7-L, C7-R
  12. 23" × 2 - Bay0-L, Bay0-R
  13. 19" × 2 - C13-L, C13-R
  14. 17-3/4" × 3 - D5, D6 + 1 spare (D5 is C7-R - already milled)
  15. Bin the pulls with their part ids on them.
The ruleOne operation on all 6 doors before the tool changes. Cut each door pair side by side from one wider blank, left then right. Cut each drawer stack from one tall blank in installed top-to-bottom order. Before separating, mark every back with its part ID, TOP and the show face; keep that face outward and do not turn parts end for end. Keep each group together through lipping and hanging. Grain continuity is across the plywood; solid lipping interrupts it at the edges.

Cut - keep the continuous groups together

  1. Open each rift sheet in Cut List. Make the numbered full-width track-saw band cuts first; then follow the lettered cuts to free each whole blank. The table supplies dimensions and the machine for every operation.
  2. Separate each pair or drawer stack as a whole rectangular blank before dividing it. Follow the diagram's grain direction and part order; do not move a leaf or front into another drop.
  3. Use the CTS for the listed rips after the blank is freed and supported. Door pairs split left-to-right on the CTS. Use the track saw for wide blank widths, drawer-stack crosscuts and island panels. Leave other groups whole until their turn, with IDs and TOP marked on their backs.
  4. These are plywood sizes - 1/8" under finished on every edge. The lipping brings them to size.
  5. Mark TOP and L/R on the back of each. Pairs stacked together.

Lip

  1. Glue TOP and BOTTOM on all 6. Never the sides first.
  2. Flush-trim the ends, all 6.
  3. Glue the long edges on all 6, full height, over the end lips.
  4. Flush-trim all 6 with the router jig. Leave a hair proud.
  5. Scrape or block-plane flush, all 6.

Shape, bore, fit

  1. Chamfer the edges on all 6, one bit height.
  2. CHECK: chamfer narrower than 1/8" on the face, or it cuts into the plywood.
  3. Sand all 6 to 150.
  4. CHECK: finished size per the Model, square.
  5. Bore hinge cups on all 6 - same jig, same reference edge.
  6. C3-R: bore to the 30C255B.08 spec sheet, not the jig setting.
  7. Fit handles on all 6 - hard stops, no measuring.

Finish and hang

  1. Oil all 6 in one session. Mix each pair's batch from the Finish table.
  2. Rack vertical to cure.
  3. Hang all 6 flush with the bottom of the frame, like C1. Set the cams.
  4. C4-L: set the side cams out, away from the trim, like C0.

Cut the prefinished drawer-box plywood

  1. Open the prefinished sheets in Cut List. Each sheet now has a numbered band plan: full-width track-saw crosscuts first, then parallel rips from the shorter panels.
  2. Follow the band table's CTS fence widths and track-saw cuts. Wide bottom panels stay on the track saw. Shorten the listed strips only after ripping; use the track saw for those crosscuts.
  3. Check both blade kerfs against the allowance printed with the plan. Label each part on its back before it leaves its band; keep grain direction as drawn.
Verify 21 measurements to tape before cutting

Tape each one, bare wood, inside edge to inside edge: W is stile to stile, H is rail to rail. Every cut size comes from these - if one is off, stop and send the number.

Kitchen — cabinet shop cut list

Export: 2026-11-02. All dimensions are inches. Size order on this export is across grain × along grain.

Includes all six replacement doors: C0-L/R, C1-L/R and C13-L/R (under sink), retaining their existing recorded sizes. This export is the remake scenario; the saved Shop Book still excludes those already-hung doors from its remaining cuts.

Island trim is square rough stock. Its three blanks include fitting length. Fit corner miters and front-end lips to the assembled wrap later. The four main island panels have vertical grain; the three bottom trim pieces have horizontal grain.

Material and quantities

  • 3/4" rift-sawn white oak plywood: 44 parts on 7 layout sheets; 67% modeled yield. Stock: 48" × 96".
  • 1/2" prefinished plywood: 40 parts on 3 layout sheets; 80% modeled yield. Stock: 48" × 96".

An optional additional spare rift sheet is outside these layouts.

Cutting requirements

  • Do not rotate parts. Grain runs along sheet length. Dimensions are shown to the nearest 1/16". Label every back with part ID, TOP and show face before separating.
  • Layout uses 1/8" kerf and 1/4" trim off each sheet edge. Group blanks include internal subdivision kerfs. With another kerf, recalculate group envelopes and nesting; individual part dimensions remain the cutting targets.
  • Each door pair comes from one contiguous blank, left then right. Each drawer-front stack comes from one contiguous tall blank in installed top-to-bottom order. Keep match groups on the same sheet and preserve orientation. The schedules on the sheet pages list whole blanks before subdivision.
  • Door/front plywood dimensions already deduct their solid lipping allowance, normally 1/8" per edge. Do not deduct it again. Island back halves have no lipping deduction.
  • Island end plywood blanks: 42-1/2" across grain × 35" along grain. Add 1/4" hardwood to front and back vertical edges only; finished edge coverage is 1-1/2" over the built-up end. Top and bottom are not lipped. Finished envelopes remain 43" × 35".
  • Bottom trim: 3/4" plywood, horizontal grain. Square rough lengths include 1" fitting allowance, based on overlay trim with front ends flush to the main ends (planning assumption). Top-edge deduction is 1/8"; add that lip to reach 5-1/2" finished height. Fit miters and exposed front ends after assembly; allow for front-end lips during fitting. Do not lip mating miter faces. Seal unlipped bottoms. Cut blank dimensions are not final miter lengths.
  • Drawer boxes use nominal 1/2" plywood. Measure actual thickness and confirm box fit before cutting; update thickness-dependent dimensions if needed. This is a panel cutting list. Grooves, runner notches, drilling, hinge boring, lipping stock, handles, island frame/build-up and seam backing are outside its scope.
